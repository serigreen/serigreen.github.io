Climate resilience for sericulture, built on measurement.

SeriGreen is an early-stage research and development company working on IoT-enabled optimisation and protection for silk farming — so that what happens to a crop can be measured, understood and acted on.

Our approach Get in touch

The problem

Silk farming is climate-exposed and largely unmeasured.

Sericulture supports a large number of smallholder families in India. It is also unusually sensitive to conditions — and unusually short of data about them.

01

Narrow tolerances

Silkworm rearing succeeds only within a tight range of environmental conditions. Excursions at the wrong stage can cost an entire crop.

02

Widening variability

Practices built around a predictable climate are losing reliability. The margin for error is narrowing season by season.

03

Little record of what happened

When a crop fails, most farms cannot say why. Without measurement there is nothing to learn from and nothing to improve against.

Our approach

Instrument the crop. Act on what the data shows.

Our work centres on bringing continuous sensing and automated response to sericulture, so conditions are managed as they change rather than discovered after the loss.

Monitor

Connected sensing through the crop cycle, capturing conditions continuously instead of depending on someone being present to observe them.

Optimise

Using what is measured to keep conditions within the range the crop needs, rather than reacting once damage is already visible.

Protect

Detecting adverse conditions early enough for intervention to still matter, and recording every cycle so outcomes can be traced to causes.

SeriGreen is pre-revenue and in active research and development. Our systems are undergoing field validation; we do not publish performance claims we cannot yet evidence.
